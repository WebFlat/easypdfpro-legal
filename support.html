Frequently asked questions

Where are my documents stored?

All documents, signatures and stamps are stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server. If you delete the app, your documents are deleted too — export important files to Files or iCloud Drive first.

How do I restore my PRO subscription?

Open Signet Scan → Settings → Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → Signet Scan → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged for the next one.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Text recognition (OCR) didn't find my text

Scan in good light, keep the page flat and fill the frame. Higher-contrast filters (Black & White) usually give the best results.

I forgot the password of an exported PDF

Passwords are not stored anywhere and cannot be recovered. Export the document again from the app — the original in the app is not password-protected.
