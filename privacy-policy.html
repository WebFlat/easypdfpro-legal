Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Easy PDF Scan ("we", "our", or "the app") is a document scanning application for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Easy PDF Scan is designed with privacy in mind. All documents, scans, signatures, and notes are stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, upload, or transmit your documents to any server.

We do not collect:

  • Personal identification information
  • Document contents or scanned images
  • Location data
  • Contact information

2. Camera and Photo Library Access

The app requests access to your device's camera and photo library solely to let you scan and import documents. This data never leaves your device.

3. In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store any payment information. Apple's privacy policy applies to all transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

4. Analytics

We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Children's Privacy

Easy PDF Scan does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app contains no features directed at children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
